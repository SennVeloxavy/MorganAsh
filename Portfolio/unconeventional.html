Unconeventional

A brand identity project for a fictional ice cream shop specializing in non-dairy desserts. This design system presents a versatile logotype and brandmark, color scheme, and type style guide for many applications. The combination of bright and natural colors help create a friendly tone, along with serif display text for a classic look. The almond shape in the logo doubles as an ice cream cone to represent the company’s focus on non-dairy desserts. The business card design, website concept, and product mockups demonstrate the identity system across print and digital media.
